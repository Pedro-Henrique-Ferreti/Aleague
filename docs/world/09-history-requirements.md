# F9 — History

## Overview

The **History** page shows the record of every past season of a World: the champion, runner-up and final standings of each tournament, one season at a time. An **Honours** view lists the champions and runners-up of each competition across all seasons. History only contains compacted seasons, so it shows stage tables, playoff brackets and winners, not individual matches.

## 1. Access and navigation

- **HIS-01** — A **History** page can be opened from the World overview.
- **HIS-02** — History lists **past seasons only**, meaning seasons that have been compacted (END-13). The current season is seen in the regular World screens.
- **HIS-03** — The user switches seasons with a **season selector** and **previous / next** arrows. The page opens on the most recent past season.
- **HIS-04** — History is **read-only**.

## 2. Season view

- **HIS-05** — For the selected season, each tournament shows its **champion** and **runner-up**, plus the **result of the final** when it had one (END-11).
- **HIS-06** — Each tournament shows its **final standings**: the champion stage's table first (CMP-18), and every other stage (e.g. each group of a group stage) through tabs. Playoff stages can also be shown as a **bracket** with the result of every tie (END-13).
- **HIS-07** — Tables keep the legends they were played with (LEG-11). Playoff tables are shown as defined in LEG-13 and LEG-14.

## 3. Honours view

- **HIS-08** — A second view, **Honours**, lists every season's **champion and runner-up** for one cup or one pyramid level at a time (e.g. Série A and Série B have separate lists).
- **HIS-09** — The Honours view also shows the **number of titles per team** in that competition.

## 4. Names

- **HIS-10** — History shows the **current** names and icons of competitions, pyramid levels and teams, even when they changed after the season was played (END-14). A tournament renamed for a specific season (CMP-10) keeps that name.
